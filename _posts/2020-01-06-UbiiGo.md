**Senior iOS Developer - Food Delivery Platform for Venezuelan Market**

Developed UbiiGo, a comprehensive food delivery application that served the Venezuelan market during the critical pandemic period. Successfully delivered a production-ready app using cutting-edge SwiftUI technology while integrating complex payment solutions and achieving significant market adoption.

**Key Impact:**
- Delivered market-ready food delivery app achieving 10,000+ downloads within first few months
- Successfully migrated from UIKit to SwiftUI during active development under aggressive deadlines
- Implemented critical shopping cart and payment gateway features for Venezuelan banking ecosystem
- Managed complete release lifecycle and production environment stabilization
- Provided essential food delivery service during pandemic-driven market demand surge

**Technical Innovation:**
- **SwiftUI Pioneer:** First professional project implementing SwiftUI, overcoming integration challenges with existing UIKit components
- **Component Architecture:** Worked with shared UI component library across multiple company projects
- **Banking Integration:** Implemented payment gateways specifically designed for Venezuelan financial institutions
- **Rapid Development:** Delivered complex features under pandemic-driven tight timelines

**Market Success & Ownership:**
- **Sep 2019 - Mar 2020:** Senior iOS Developer with full production responsibility
- **Launch Impact:** 5,000+ downloads in first month, scaling to 10,000+ total downloads
- **Production Support:** Provided 3-month post-launch support ensuring platform stability
- **Technical Leadership:** Served as iOS technical reference for all app-related decisions

This project showcased my ability to rapidly adopt emerging technologies (SwiftUI) while delivering business-critical applications under pressure, demonstrating both technical expertise and market-responsive development skills in the growing food delivery sector.
