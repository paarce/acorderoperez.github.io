**Technical Lead - Gamified Retail Engagement Platform**

Led the complete technical development of WinQuest, an innovative AR-powered treasure hunt mobile application that bridges gaming and retail through immersive experiences. Successfully delivered a cross-platform solution connecting users with popular retail brands through gamified discount discovery.

**Key Impact:**
- Architected and delivered full-stack AR gaming platform from concept to Friends & Family release
- Pioneered AR implementation using emerging ARKit/ARCore technologies with Unity integration
- Built sophisticated in-game economy with real-world currency exchange and transaction tracking
- Led multidisciplinary team across iOS, Android, Web, and backend development
- Delivered complex features including in-app purchases, leaderboards, and AR treasure experiences

**Technical Leadership Journey:**
- **Mar 2018 - Sep 2019:** Technical Lead supporting Product Owner with hands-on iOS development
- **Platform Scope:** Complete ecosystem spanning native mobile apps, web frontend, and Node.js API
- **Innovation Focus:** Early adoption of mobile AR technologies when documentation was scarce

This project showcased my ability to lead technical innovation in emerging technologies while managing complex cross-platform development and building scalable gaming-retail hybrid solutions.
