**Tech Lead & Infrastructure Engineer - Immersive Business Platform**

Led development of Mytaverse, an innovative platform enabling businesses to connect with customers and empower their workforce through immersive 3D virtual environments. Successfully transitioned from web team leadership to infrastructure automation, delivering scalable VR solutions for enterprise clients.

**Key Impact:**
- Architected and delivered voice/video streaming capabilities for VR events supporting 500+ simultaneous users
- Built multi-merchant platform enabling automated server provisioning and peripheral management
- Led cross-functional team of 6 developers and 1 QA through critical platform features
- Implemented infrastructure-as-code solutions using Terraform for scalable AWS deployments
- Drove revenue growth through merchant expansion model (7 merchants hosting 36+ events annually)

**Technical Leadership Journey:**
- **2021 Q4:** Led web development team focusing on platform core features
- **2022 Q1-Q3:** Transitioned to infrastructure team, specializing in automation and DevOps

This project demonstrated my versatility in both technical leadership and infrastructure engineering, delivering cutting-edge VR solutions that bridge the gap between virtual experiences and business outcomes.
