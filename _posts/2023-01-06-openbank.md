In the dynamic realm of fintech, my pivotal role in the "Zinia - Openbank" project showcases my expertise in crafting innovative solutions. Spearheading this experimental endeavor for a prominent bank, I seamlessly integrated cutting-edge Apple technologies—SwiftUI, async-await—into a bespoke architecture. 

Navigating the intricate landscape of financial regulations, my contributions empowered the implementation of Zinia, a service revolutionizing customer purchases through installment payments. The project not only pushed technological boundaries but also underscored my commitment to user-centric design and adaptability in an ever-evolving financial ecosystem. This experience epitomizes my prowess in leveraging the latest iOS tools to drive impactful and forward-thinking solutions.
