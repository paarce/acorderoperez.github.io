Based on your current portfolio content, here are the key questions you should prepare to enhance your professional
  presentation:

  Technical Leadership & Team Management

  - Team Size & Scope: What was the exact team composition you led? (iOS devs, QA, designers, PMs)
  - Leadership Style: How do you mentor junior developers? What's your approach to code reviews?
  - Cross-functional Collaboration: Specific examples of resolving conflicts between teams (iOS vs Backend, Design vs
  Engineering)
  - Technical Decisions: What major architectural decisions did you make and why? What were the trade-offs?

  Quantifiable Impact & Metrics

  - User Base: How many active users did your apps serve? (DAU/MAU)
  - Performance Improvements: Specific metrics (app launch time, crash rates, conversion rates)
  - Business Impact: Revenue generated, cost savings, user retention improvements
  - Development Efficiency: How much did you reduce development time/costs? Code reusability metrics?
  - App Store Performance: Download numbers, ratings, featured placements

  FinTech Expertise Depth

  - Regulatory Compliance: Which financial regulations did you implement? (PCI DSS, GDPR, PSD2)
  - Security Measures: Specific security implementations (biometric auth, encryption, fraud detection)
  - Payment Integrations: Which payment providers/APIs did you integrate? (Stripe, PayPal, bank APIs)
  - Financial Products: Types of financial services you've built (loans, payments, investments, banking)

  Technical Challenges & Problem Solving

  - Complex Problems: What was the most challenging technical problem you solved?
  - Performance Optimization: Specific examples of memory/battery/network optimizations
  - Scalability: How did you handle growing user bases? Architecture evolution?
  - Legacy Code: How did you modernize legacy systems? Migration strategies?

  International & Cultural Adaptation

  - Market Differences: How did you adapt apps for different markets (Spain vs Germany vs LATAM)?
  - Localization Challenges: Beyond language - cultural UX differences, local regulations
  - Remote Team Management: How did you manage distributed teams across time zones?

  Career Progression & Growth

  - Promotion Path: How did you evolve from developer to Tech Lead? Key milestones?
  - Skill Development: How do you stay current with iOS technologies? Learning approach?
  - Mentoring Impact: How many developers have you mentored? Their career progression?
  - Project Ownership: Evolution from contributor to owner - specific examples

  Industry Knowledge & Vision

  - FinTech Trends: Your perspective on the future of mobile financial services
  - Technology Adoption: Why did you choose SwiftUI early? How do you evaluate new technologies?
  - Market Understanding: Insights about different financial markets you've worked in

  Specific Project Deep-Dives

  For each major project, prepare:
  - Problem Statement: What business problem were you solving?
  - Technical Approach: Why this architecture/technology choice?
  - Obstacles Overcome: Major blockers and how you solved them
  - Results Achieved: Quantified outcomes and business impact
  - Lessons Learned: What you'd do differently, what worked well

  Soft Skills & Leadership

  - Stakeholder Management: How do you communicate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders?
  - Priority Management: How do you balance technical debt vs new features?
  - Crisis Management: Examples of handling production issues, tight deadlines
  - Cultural Intelligence: Working effectively across Venezuelan, Spanish, and German cultures

  Future Aspirations

  - Career Goals: Where do you see yourself in 3-5 years?
  - Technology Interests: What emerging technologies excite you?
  - Industry Focus: Do you want to stay in FinTech or explore other domains?
  - Leadership Growth: Aspirations for larger teams, broader technical influence

  Personal Differentiators

  - Unique Value Proposition: What makes you different from other iOS Tech Leads?
  - Cross-Cultural Advantage: How does your Venezuelan-Spanish background benefit teams?
  - Hobby Integration: How do your interests (DJ, investments, Russian) relate to your professional skills?
